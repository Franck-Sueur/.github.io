Start with the Lax–Milgram theorem
About these notes

This volume gathers a sequence of chapters on the analysis and numerical approximation of partial differential equations. It begins with the Lax–Milgram theorem, energy minimization, and the Stampacchia theorem for constrained variational problems. Sobolev spaces then provide the framework for elliptic boundary-value problems and conforming finite element methods in one and two space dimensions. The Lax–Richtmyer chapter introduces consistency, stability, and convergence immediately before their applications to finite difference approximations of first-order hyperbolic equations. It includes both a fixed-space operator result and an evolution theorem on changing grid spaces. Exercises accompany the theoretical and numerical developments, and a final chapter gathers their worked solutions.
